Saath Community Guidelines

Saath is an 18+ community. Treat every person with respect and help keep the platform safe.

Be genuine

Use your own identity and recent, representative photos. Do not impersonate others or create fake profiles.

Respect consent

Respect boundaries. No harassment, threats, stalking, unwanted sexual messages or sharing intimate content without consent.

No scams or exploitation

Do not request money, OTPs, passwords, bank details or gifts through deceptive or coercive behaviour. Do not promote scams, phishing or fraudulent schemes.

Keep content lawful and appropriate

No hate, threats, exploitation, sexual content involving minors, non-consensual intimate imagery, illegal content or content that violates another person's rights.

Protect privacy

Do not publish another person's private contact details, address, workplace, documents or private conversations without permission.

Report and block

Use the in-app Report and Block tools when someone makes you uncomfortable or violates these rules. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services.

Moderation

Saath may review reports and take action, including content removal, restrictions, suspension or account termination. Serious or repeated violations may result in permanent removal.
