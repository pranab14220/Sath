Saath Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Saath is an 18+ dating and social discovery service. This policy explains the personal information handled when you use the Saath website or Android app.

1. Who operates Saath

Saath is currently operated independently by its developer. For privacy questions or requests, contact mpranab14220@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Sign-in information: authentication information supplied through Google Sign-In, such as your account identifier and email address, as made available by Google.
  • Profile information: name/display name, age, gender, who you are interested in, state, city, biography and profile photo that you choose to provide.
  • Service activity: likes, matches, chat messages, blocks, reports, and information needed to operate account features, including message read status and app preferences.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting support.

3. Information we do not request through the current profile

The current profile asks you to enter your state and city. The app does not request GPS coordinates or continuous precise location through its current profile flow. Do not include sensitive personal information in your bio or messages.

4. How we use information

  • Create and maintain your account and profile.
  • Show profiles and support likes, matches and conversations.
  • Operate safety features, investigate reports, enforce our rules and prevent abuse.
  • Maintain message-read status and troubleshoot errors.
  • Respond to support requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

5. When information is shared

Your chosen profile details and photos may be visible to other Saath users as part of discovery and matching. Messages are visible to the conversation participants and may be reviewed when necessary to investigate a report or safety concern. Reports are available to authorised moderation personnel. We do not sell personal information.

6. Service providers

Saath uses Supabase for backend services such as authentication, database and photo storage. Google is used for Google Sign-In. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy policies. Saath may disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or others.

7. Storage and security

Information is stored using the service infrastructure configured for Saath. We use reasonable measures intended to protect it, but no internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your Google account secure and never share passwords or one-time passcodes.

8. Retention and deletion

You can use the in-app Delete Profile option to request deletion of your Saath profile data and related app records handled by the account-deletion process. The current in-app confirmation states that your Google login identity remains and is not deleted by this action. Some information, such as records needed for safety, dispute handling, security or legal compliance, may be retained where permitted or required by law. For help with a privacy request, email mpranab14220@gmail.com.

9. Your choices and rights

You can choose what profile details and photos to add, edit your profile, block or report users, stop using the service, and request deletion through the app or by contacting us. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights to access, correct, erase or object to certain processing. We will handle valid requests under applicable law.

10. Children

Saath is intended only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly permit anyone under 18 to use the service. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

11. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than where you live. Where required, we take steps intended to ensure appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as Saath changes or legal requirements evolve. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use after an update is subject to the revised policy to the extent permitted by law.

13. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: mpranab14220@gmail.com.
